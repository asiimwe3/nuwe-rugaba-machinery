Frequently Asked Questions

What buyers ask before ordering

Where can I buy water pumps and generators in Fort Portal?

At Nuwe Rugaba Machinery on Fort Portal City. Machine shop on Moldina Street. We supply submersible and solar submersible pumps, high-pressure water pumps, diesel and petrol generators, MIG/TIG/gas welding machines, silage and hay machines, earth augers, grass cutters, concrete mixers and construction machinery. Message us on WhatsApp at 0782 461 461.

Do you deliver machinery outside Fort Portal?

Yes. We deliver machinery countrywide across Uganda, including mining sites, farms, construction projects and water supply schemes.

What kind of machinery does Nuwe Rugaba Machinery sell?

Water pumps (submersible, high-head and surface pumps), diesel and petrol generators, concrete mixers and vibrators, welding machines, air compressors, farm and irrigation equipment, spare parts and servicing.

How do I get a price for a machine?

Tap Request Price on any product to message us directly on WhatsApp, or call 0782 461 461. We quote by your specifications — pump head, power rating and duty requirements.

Is Nuwe Rugaba Machinery the largest machinery wholesale shop in Western Uganda?

Yes. From our base in Fort Portal City on Moldina Street, we run the largest machinery wholesale and retail operation in Western Uganda — water pumps, generators, construction machinery, workshop equipment, farm machines and spare parts all stocked under one roof, with trade and wholesale pricing for resellers, contractors, NGOs and institutions.

Do you offer wholesale and trade pricing for shops and resellers?

Yes. We supply shops, hardware stores, contractors, farms, mining companies, NGOs and water schemes at wholesale and trade prices on bulk machine orders. Ask us on WhatsApp at 0782 461 461 for a wholesale quotation.

Which towns and districts in Western Uganda do you deliver machinery to?

We deliver machinery across all of Western Uganda — Fort Portal City, Kasese, Kyenjojo, Kyegegwa, Bundibugyo, Ntoroko, Kamwenge, Bunyangabu, Rubirizi, Kagadi, Kibaale, Hoima, Mubende, Masindi, Mbarara, Bushenyi and Ibanda — plus countrywide delivery anywhere in Uganda, including mining sites, farms, construction projects and water supply schemes.
